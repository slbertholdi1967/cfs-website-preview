    GMU - Institute for Digital Innovation

    Arlington, VA

    CFS Designs US delivered a stunning full-building façade for the George Mason University – Institute for Digital Innovation. The project featured two custom Alucobond finishes — Sunstorm Pewter and Arcadia Silver with grain — paired with ATAS RigidWall II panels in two additional custom colors. Our scope included the fabrication of intricate architectural eyebrows and full cladding of the sky bridge. This high-profile project showcases CFS’s commitment to precision, innovation, and exceptional design execution.
