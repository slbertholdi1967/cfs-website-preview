    Sustainability

    Better walls, less waste.

    A rainscreen is one of the most durable ways to clad a building. Here is how we help your project meet its goals.

    Ventilated, insulated walls

    A rainscreen keeps the insulation outside the structure and lets the cavity behind the panels drain and dry, which helps the wall manage moisture and energy.

    Recyclable materials

    Aluminum can be recycled again and again without losing its properties, and the metal in our panels, trims and subframes can be recovered at the end of the building's life.

    Documents for your submittals

    Ask us for the manufacturer documentation your project needs: Environmental Product Declarations (EPDs), LEED data sheets and recycled content letters.
