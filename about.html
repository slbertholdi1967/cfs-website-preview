    About us

    Since 2017, building the East Coast's facades.

    Since 2017, CFS Designs US has been the premier fabricator of ACM, Fiber Cement, and miscellaneous metal facades for the East Coast. Our 100,000 square foot facility allows us to handle all aspects of fabrication and distribution, ensuring a smooth and efficient experience for our clients.

    With a team of engineers, builders, architects, and draftsmen boasting over 100 years of combined construction expertise, we can tackle any project, from assisting with the design phase to flawless execution.

    Our vision is to provide design solutions to our clients: high quality interior and exterior products from market leaders around the world, plus shop drawing, engineering and design assistance services. This full spectrum approach brings value that results in lasting relationships.
